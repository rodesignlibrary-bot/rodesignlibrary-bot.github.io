Selection Guidelines
Coarse bubble — preferred for EQ tanks & sludge holding. Provides mixing & agitation. Lower oxygen transfer efficiency but clog-resistant. Suitable where mixing is primary goal.
Fine bubble — preferred for aeration tanks. Higher OTE (18–35%), smaller bubbles = greater surface area. Disc diffusers: 7″ & 9″ for normal aeration; 12″ for high-demand zones. Requires clean air & periodic maintenance.
